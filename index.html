Tiny typing tip

Home row = home base 🏡

Rest your left fingers on A, S, D, F and your right fingers on J, K, L, ;. Let your thumbs rest near the space bar. You can peek at the keyboard while learning.

🌱 Progress, not perfectionIt's okay to make mistakes! Slow and steady helps your fingers learn where each key lives.
